Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Eazzio ("we", "our") collects when a hotel signs up for and uses the Eazzio Hotel Management System (the "Service"), and how that information is stored, used, and shared.

1. Who this applies to

The Service is used by three kinds of people: Admins (hotel owners/managers who create an account and subscribe), Staff (accounts an Admin creates for their Room, Banquet, or Restaurant team), and Guests (the Admin's or Staff's own customers, whose details get entered into the system as part of a booking or order). This policy covers all three.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone, and a securely hashed password (or your Google account identifier, if you sign in with Google) for Admins; name, email, phone, and a hashed password for Staff accounts an Admin creates.
  • Guest information entered by you: guest name, phone, email, and ID proof type/number for room bookings; customer name and phone for banquet and restaurant bookings. This is entered by your hotel's Admin or Staff, not collected by us directly from guests.
  • Business data: rooms, room categories, pricing, banquet halls, menu items, invoices, and similar records you create while using the Service.
  • Payment information: subscription payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card, UPI, or bank account details — Razorpay handles that directly and shares back only a payment confirmation and ID.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs (e.g. error logs) needed to keep the Service running reliably.

3. How we use this information

We use the information above only to operate the Service: authenticating logins, enforcing which department a Staff account can access, generating invoices and reports, and — for Premium plans — powering the AI Assistant and Voice Commands features. We do not sell any information to third parties, and we do not use guest data for advertising.

4. The AI Assistant (Premium plans only)

If your plan includes the AI Assistant, questions you ask it are sent — along with a summary of your hotel's own aggregated numbers (revenue, occupancy, top-selling items, and similar figures, not individual guest records) — to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use that feature.

5. Where data is stored

Your data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase, and photos/documents you upload (room photos, banquet hall photos, invoice PDFs) are stored in Supabase Storage. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they're hashed with bcrypt before saving.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email us at eazziohotel@gmail.com and we'll process the request, subject to any records we're required to keep for legal or accounting purposes (for example, GST invoice records).

7. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits (a login token) and to remember minor preferences. We don't use third-party advertising cookies or trackers.

8. Children's data

The Service is intended for business use by hotel owners, managers, and staff, and is not directed at children. Guest ID proof details entered into the system are handled with the same security measures as all other guest data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to eazziohotel@gmail.com.
