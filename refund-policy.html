Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Creating an Eazzio account is free — you're never charged until you choose a plan and complete payment from your dashboard. This page explains what happens if you want to cancel or ask for a refund afterward.

1. Cancellation

Subscriptions do not auto-renew (see our Terms of Service), so there's nothing to actively "cancel" to stop future charges — your plan simply runs for its 12-month term and then expires unless you choose to renew. If you'd like your account deactivated or deleted before then, email us at eazziohotel@gmail.com.

2. Refund eligibility

Because a subscription unlocks full access to your dashboard immediately, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis rather than guaranteed automatically. We'll generally offer a full refund if:

  • You were charged due to a genuine technical error (for example, a duplicate payment or a payment that succeeded but your plan never activated), or
  • You request a refund within 7 days of payment and have not materially used the paid features (for example, you upgraded to Premium but never used the AI Assistant or Voice Commands).
Note for the site owner: this 7-day window and the conditions above are a reasonable starting default — adjust them to whatever refund policy you actually intend to run before relying on this page.

3. How to request a refund

Email eazziohotel@gmail.com with the email address on your account and the approximate date of payment. We'll confirm the payment against our Razorpay records and let you know the outcome, typically within a few business days.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Depending on your bank, it can take several business days to reflect after we initiate it.

5. Plan upgrades

If you upgrade mid-term (for example, from Restaurant Only to All Departments), you're only charged the price difference, and your existing plan's expiry date carries over — an upgrade payment follows the same refund conditions above.

6. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to eazziohotel@gmail.com.
